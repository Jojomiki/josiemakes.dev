ART + WORDS + THE UNIVERSE

STARS✦

An experiment in optimism, possibility, and the things we don't always see.

I paired astronomical imagery with words that made me think about what it means to be tiny in an ever-expanding universe — and still capable of making something, learning something, helping someone, or changing what happens next.

Each piece pairs an image of the universe with a quote, alongside a companion page exploring the object itself.

Someday, maybe a coffee-table book. For now, a little corner of the internet. ✦
